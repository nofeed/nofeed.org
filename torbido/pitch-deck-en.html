02

The Problem

1,000+ microbreweries and 800+ artisan distilleries in Italy produce exceptional beverages, yet remain invisible online.

  • No aggregator marketplace — each producer has their own site (when one exists at all), zero discoverability
  • Unmet demand — 68% of consumers under 40 look for local products within 50 km but can't find them online
  • Eroded margins — distributors and wine shops take 40–60% of the margin; producers have no alternative
  • Immature market — wine already has Tannico, Callmewine, Vivino. Craft beer and artisan spirits have nobody

440 new craft beers registered in Italy in 2025. Almost none can be easily purchased online directly from the producer.

03

The Solution

Torbido is the pure marketplace (Etsy model) for craft beer and local spirits.

For Producers

  • Personal storefront with territorial storytelling
  • Direct-to-consumer sales, no middlemen
  • Competitive commissions (5–12% vs 40–60% distributors)
  • Zero fixed costs, zero inventory
  • Simple tools to manage catalogue and orders
  • Torbido Print — free printable profile PDF; or bound copies (softcover/hardcover) for events, taprooms, and client gifting (€18–35/copy)

For Consumers

  • Built-in "km 0" geographic filter
  • Discover hidden local producers
  • Territory stories behind every bottle
  • Buy directly from the producer
  • Browse by style (dedicated pages), ingredient, territory

Pure marketplace model: Torbido holds no inventory, handles no logistics, does not compete with producers. Infinite scalability.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Phase 1: Lombardy Pilot Launch (Months 0–8)

  • Onboarding 15–20 producers (mix of breweries + distilleries) — direct outreach, concentrated territory
  • Target: 200 registered users, product-market fit validation
  • Focus: first 10 completed orders, feedback collection, UX optimisation

Phase 2: Italy Traction + International Prep (Months 8–12)

  • 40–50 active producers, expansion to Piedmont/Veneto/Emilia
  • Target: 1,000+ registered users, €15–25K monthly GMV
  • Platform localisation kick-off (DE/EN), national domain registration (torbido.de, torbido.at, torbido.be), pre-outreach to 10 DACH breweries, cross-border distribution partners identified

Phase 3: Italy + DACH Launch in Parallel (Months 12–18)

  • Italy track: national coverage, 200+ producers, SEO dominance — completed by month 18
  • DACH track (simultaneous from month 12): soft launch in Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium on national domains — language and local products follow the domain
  • Italian producers visible cross-border + onboarding 20–30 local DACH producers
  • DACH target: first international transactions, €5–10K GMV/month abroad by month 18

Phase 4: Secondary Europe — France, Spain, Netherlands (Year 2–3)

  • Scale DACH to 100+ local producers
  • Enter France, Spain, Netherlands: fast-growing craft markets, all Eurozone — zero additional regulatory requirements
  • Localisation FR/ES/NL on national domains; team of 5–8
  • Target: total GMV €5–7M, 350+ EU producers, DR 35–45

Phase 5: Emerging Europe — Poland, Czech Rep., Romania (Year 3–4)

  • Fast-growing craft markets, lower operating costs, zero digital competitors in the segment
  • Czech Rep.: oldest brewing culture in Europe. Poland: +40% craft growth in the last 3 years
  • All EU countries — same e-commerce regulations, same OSS VAT
  • Year 4 target: GMV €10–15M, 600+ EU producers

Phase 6: Extra-EU Markets — USA, UK, Japan, Australia, Canada (Year 4–5+)

  • Only after EU consolidation: proven playbook, 6–8 active markets, Series A closed
  • USA: single pilot state (Oregon/Vermont), Three-Tier System via licensed local partners
  • UK post-Brexit: separate customs and licensing vs EU
  • Year 5 target: GMV €20–30M, 1,000+ global producers, 10+ markets
